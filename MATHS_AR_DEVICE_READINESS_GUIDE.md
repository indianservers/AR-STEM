# Maths AR Device Readiness Guide

## Checks

Readiness includes ARCore support and install state, camera permission, depth support, environmental HDR, flash support, local Wi-Fi, LAN discovery, battery, storage, thermal state, map readiness, offline map availability, location readiness and accessibility settings.

## Output

Reports use device aliases rather than permanent identifiers. They are intended for local classroom readiness planning and should not include raw imagery or precise location history.

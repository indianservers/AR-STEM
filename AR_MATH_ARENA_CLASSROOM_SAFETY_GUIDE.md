# AR Math Arena Classroom Safety Guide

## Room Preparation

- Clear a 5 metre play area when using active AR movement.
- Keep walking paths away from desks, cables and walls.
- Use seated or non-AR analyst mode for students who need low-motion participation.
- Avoid running, pushing or looking only through the device while moving.

## Device Preparation

- Charge devices before play.
- Check camera permission and ARCore status before students join.
- Use high contrast or large text when needed.
- Reduce motion and particles on lower-end devices.

## Live Safety Controls

The host can pause the match, end the match, evaluate boundaries and lower difficulty. The game supports local-only play, so no student camera feed leaves the device through this feature.

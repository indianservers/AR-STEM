# Maths AR Known Limitations

- Physical ARCore tracking, geospatial VPS coverage and real device depth quality still require device-field QA.
- Content-pack archive serialization is represented by a deterministic local payload and checksum contract; production archive signing can be added on top.
- Teacher Authoring Studio currently exposes the authoring workflow and validators with sample local content rather than a persistent database-backed editor.
- Release performance numbers are static audit estimates until measured on a device matrix.
- Outdoor Math Expedition route authoring requires school safety review before live use.

# Maths AR Tournament Guide

## Supported Event Types

The tournament hub supports single-game events, mixed-game championships, team leagues, cooperative class quests, skill decathlons and custom teacher-created events.

## Authoring Integration

Tournament presets from Teacher Authoring Studio define game mix, rounds, team size, scoring weights, tie-break rules, accommodations, spectator limit and replay setting.

## Scoring

Score weights are validated to total `1.0`. Results require host/teacher confirmation and tie-breaks are deterministic by default.

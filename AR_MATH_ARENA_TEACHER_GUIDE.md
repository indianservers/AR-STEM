# AR Math Arena Teacher Guide

## Setup

1. Print the AR Math Arena origin marker from `docs/ar_math_arena_origin_marker_2400.png`.
2. Put all devices on the same local Wi-Fi.
3. Open Games Library, choose AR Math Arena, and run AR Device Check.
4. Host creates a room and students join with QR text or local discovery.
5. Assign teams and roles, then calibrate the shared origin.

## Roles

- Navigator: interprets spatial clues.
- Solver: performs calculations.
- Builder: places and upgrades defences.
- Analyst: checks patterns and can participate without AR.
- Commander: coordinates team choices.

## During Play

Use Teacher Mode or Teacher Dashboard to pause, adapt difficulty, toggle accessibility settings, check boundaries, end the match and create a local summary. The app is built for direct local classroom play and does not require a teacher cloud dashboard.

## After Play

Generate the local match summary and CSV preview. Do not treat the current build as classroom-ready until physical AR and two-device LAN validation are completed.

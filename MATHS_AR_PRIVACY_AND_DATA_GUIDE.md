# Maths AR Privacy And Data Guide

## Local-First Data

Teacher authored missions, presets, local school profile defaults and backups are modeled as local app data. No upload path is introduced by Phase 6.

## Diagnostics

Support diagnostics must exclude camera imagery, precise location history, student display names and IP history by default.

## Replays

Tournament replay stores gameplay events and score changes, not raw camera video.

## Outdoor Routes

Math Expedition route authoring uses approved route definitions and map metadata. Student location history is not required for content-pack authoring.
